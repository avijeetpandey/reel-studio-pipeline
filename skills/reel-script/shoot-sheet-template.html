LinkedInPost the morning after, Tue–Thu 8–10 AM (your timezone)

Most AI mistakes I review aren't model bugs. They're trust bugs. A language model doesn't check facts by default. It predicts the next word that is most likely to fit, one word at a time. That's why it can write a confident paragraph, with a citation, that is completely wrong. Researchers call this a hallucination. To the model, "sounds right" and "is right" look the same. What this means for engineering teams: • Treat AI output like a pull request from a new teammate. Review it before it ships. • Ask for sources, then open them. • Use retrieval or tools for facts.
